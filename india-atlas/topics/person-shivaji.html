Shivaji

1630–1680 · Founder of the Maratha kingdom

Born at Shivneri, he took Torna in 1646, built a hill-fort kingdom against Bijapur and the Mughals, escaped from Agra in 1666, and was crowned Chhatrapati at Raigad in 1674.

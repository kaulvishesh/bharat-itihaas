Xuanzang

602–664 · Chinese Buddhist pilgrim and translator

Travelled from Chang'an to India (629–645), studied at Nalanda under Silabhadra, toured the subcontinent and returned with 657 Buddhist texts. His Records of the Western Regions is a prime source for 7th-century India.

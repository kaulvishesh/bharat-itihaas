Today · South Asia today

Seven decades on: India, Pakistan, Bangladesh and their neighbours.

Bangladesh emerged in 1971 after a war of liberation. India liberalised its economy in 1991, and since then services and technology have grown rapidly; the country has also seen new states created (Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Uttarakhand in 2000; Telangana in 2014). Several land and maritime boundaries, notably in Kashmir and along the Himalayan frontier, remain disputed or undemarcated; this map takes no position on them.

Key events

1971 · Bangladesh Liberation War

East Pakistan secedes after a nine-month war; Bangladesh is born. — Dhaka

1991 · Economic liberalisation

A balance-of-payments crisis triggers reforms that dismantle most of the "licence raj". — New Delhi

2000 · Three new states formed

Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand and Uttarakhand are carved out of Madhya Pradesh, Bihar and Uttar Pradesh. — Dehradun (Uttarakhand)

2014 · Telangana formed

India's 29th state is carved out of Andhra Pradesh, with Hyderabad as the shared capital for ten years. — Hyderabad

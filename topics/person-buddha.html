Gautama Buddha

c. 563–483 BCE (traditional) · Founder of Buddhism

Born a Shakya prince at Lumbini, he left home at 29, attained enlightenment at Bodh Gaya, taught for about 45 years across the Gangetic plain, and died at Kushinagar. Some scholars date his life about a century later.

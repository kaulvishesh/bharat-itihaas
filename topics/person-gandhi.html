Mohandas K. Gandhi

1869–1948 · Leader of the Indian independence movement

Born at Porbandar, he returned from South Africa in 1915, led satyagrahas at Champaran and Ahmedabad, the Salt March of 1930, and the Quit India movement; he was assassinated in Delhi in 1948.

Adi Shankara

traditionally 788–820 · Philosopher of Advaita Vedanta

Traditional accounts say he walked from Kerala across India debating scholars, wrote commentaries on the Upanishads, Gita and Brahma Sutras, and founded four mathas. Scholars debate the dates and details.

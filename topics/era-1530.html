1526 CE · Vijayanagara, Deccan Sultanates & the dawn of the Mughals

Regional powers flourish as Babur enters from the north-west.

Krishnadevaraya's Vijayanagara controlled the south, while five successor states of the Bahmani Sultanate — Bijapur, Ahmadnagar, Golkonda, Bidar, Berar — dominated the Deccan. In the north, Babur used cannon and matchlocks to defeat Ibrahim Lodi at Panipat (1526), then Rana Sanga at Khanwa (1527). Europeans arrived by sea: the Portuguese took Goa in 1510. Key events

1498 · Vasco da Gama reaches Calicut

The first direct sea route from Europe to India opens the era of European maritime trade. — Calicut (Kozhikode)

1510 · Portuguese capture Goa

Afonso de Albuquerque seizes Goa from Bijapur, creating a Portuguese base that lasts until 1961. — Goa

1526 · First Battle of Panipat

Babur defeats Ibrahim Lodi using field artillery and founds the Mughal Empire. — Panipat

1527 · Battle of Khanwa

Babur defeats the Rajput confederacy led by Rana Sanga, securing Mughal control of north India. — Khanwa

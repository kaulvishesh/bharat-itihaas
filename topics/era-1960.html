1950 CE · Independence & the Republic

Independence in 1947, Partition, integration of princely states, and a new Constitution.

Partition displaced an estimated 10–20 million people and killed perhaps one to two million. Sardar Patel and V. P. Menon persuaded or pressed over 560 princely states to accede; Junagadh, Hyderabad and Kashmir were contested. The Constitution (adopted 26 Nov 1949, in force 26 Jan 1950) created a federal parliamentary republic; the States Reorganisation Act (1956) redrew states on linguistic lines. Key events

15 Aug 1947 · Independence and Partition

British rule ends; the subcontinent is partitioned into India and Pakistan amid mass migration and violence. — New Delhi

1948 · Hyderabad integrated

Operation Polo brings the Nizam's state into the Indian Union. — Hyderabad

26 Jan 1950 · The Constitution comes into force

India becomes a sovereign democratic republic; 26 January is celebrated as Republic Day. — New Delhi

1956 · States Reorganisation

Indian states are redrawn on linguistic lines after the Andhra agitation and the States Reorganisation Commission. — Hyderabad / Telangana region

1783 CE · Marathas, Mysore & the Company

Mughal power fragments; successor states and the East India Company compete.

After Nader Shah's sack of Delhi (1739) and Plassey (1757), power moved to regional rulers. The Marathas dominated central India until Panipat (1761) checked them in the north; Hyder Ali and Tipu Sultan built a militarised state in Mysore; the Nizam, Nawab of Awadh and Sikh misls ruled the rest. The Company acquired Bengal's diwani in 1765. The Treaty of Salbai (1782) ended the first Anglo-Maratha war.

Key events

1757 · Battle of Plassey

Robert Clive defeats Siraj ud-Daulah, the Nawab of Bengal, laying the foundation for Company rule. — Plassey (Palashi)

1761 · Third Battle of Panipat

Ahmad Shah Durrani defeats the Maratha army, halting Maratha expansion in the north. — Panipat

1765 · Company obtains the diwani of Bengal

After Buxar, the Mughal emperor grants the East India Company the right to collect revenue in Bengal, Bihar and Orissa. — Allahabad

1782 · Treaty of Salbai

Ends the First Anglo-Maratha War: territory the British took after the Treaty of Purandar (1776) is returned and Maratha power is recognised. — Salbai

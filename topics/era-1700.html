1700 CE · Mughal Empire at its largest

Aurangzeb's empire stretches over almost the whole subcontinent — while the Marathas contest it.

Around 1700 the Mughals ruled an estimated quarter of the world's manufacturing output, but their 25-year Deccan war drained the treasury. Maratha guerrilla warfare, Rajput and Sikh revolts, and the rise of regional governors prepared the empire's fragmentation. The dashed green area marks the Maratha heartland and is only a rough sketch; its edges were fluid.

Key events

1653 · Taj Mahal completed

Shah Jahan's white-marble mausoleum for Mumtaz Mahal, built over about two decades on the Yamuna. — Agra

1674 · Shivaji crowned Chhatrapati

Shivaji is crowned at his fortress capital, formalising an independent Maratha kingdom. — Raigad Fort

1690 · Calcutta founded

The English East India Company establishes a trading post at Sutanuti on the Hooghly, which grows into Calcutta. — Kolkata (Calcutta)

1699 · Khalsa founded

Guru Gobind Singh founds the Khalsa order at Anandpur Sahib on Baisakhi. — Anandpur Sahib
